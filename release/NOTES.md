The Raw Ham's Lite Logger 2.02 - PC 2.02 / Android 2.02

More fixes from real time on the air.

New in 2.02:
- Tap or click a spot and the cursor lands in RST Sent with the 59 picked. Just type over it.
- Send QSLs shows the card(s) that will go out, and has a "Send a test to me first" button.
- The main choice in every window is orange, so it's easy to find. Continue is first in Past activations.
- Mark NOT sent: take the QSL, QRZ, log of choice or LoTW mark off a contact so you can fix it and send again.
- Test LoTW in Settings (PC): checks TQSL and your station location without uploading anything.
- Fixed: sent marks now stay with your saved files, so Continue and Pick up don't send things twice.
- Tablet: the screen stays on while QSLs go out, and each ham is marked the moment their email goes.

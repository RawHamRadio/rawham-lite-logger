The Raw Ham's Lite Logger 2.0 - PC 2.0 / Android 2.0

Version 2 is here! Still free, still lite.

New in 2.0:
- QSLs by email: one tap after an activation and every ham gets their own QSL email, with your card, up to 4 activation photos and a map. Email lookup through QRZ or free HamQTH.
- Map cards: your activation map made into a QSL card with each contact on it. Or make your own card from any picture. Print them 3 to a page.
- Send QSL to just one ham, and (optional) send each contact to QRZ and its QSL the moment you log it.
- Move between your PC and tablet through your own Google Drive or Dropbox: activations and settings. Free, no account.
- Power read from your radio, a heads-up if you're outside your license privileges, and directions to the park.
- Change many contacts at once, make a new activator their own log under their call with a click, and open any ADIF.

- Windows: Raw_Ham_Lite_Logger_Setup.exe (installs over 1.x, your log stays)
- Android 8+: RawHamLiteLogger.apk (installs over 1.x, your log stays)
- Linux / Raspberry Pi: Raw_Ham_Lite_Logger_Linux.zip
- Guide: https://github.com/RawHamRadio/rawham-lite-logger/raw/main/Guide.pdf

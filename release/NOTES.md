The Raw Ham's Lite Logger 2.01 - PC 2.01 / Android 2.01

A small fix-up after 2.0, from my own time on the air with it.

New in 2.01:
- Tap or click a spot and the ham's photo shows right away.
- The station you pick in WSJT-X shows in the Call box before you log it (name, worked before, photo).
- License check now counts where your sideband really sits. 7.175 LSB as a General reaches 7.172, so you get a red heads-up. Still never blocks you.
- Out of your privileges: a bold red banner, plus a red band and a warning sign. Now on the tablet too, live as you tune.
- Freq and Mode moved down next to Pwr, on PC and tablet.
- Spots: an orange strip tells you when your Band, Mode or Show picks are hiding spots, with one tap to show them all.
- Tablet fix: new spots were showing up blank in light mode. They show now.

- Windows: Raw_Ham_Lite_Logger_Setup.exe (installs over 2.0, your log stays)
- Android 8+: RawHamLiteLogger.apk (installs over 2.0, your log stays)
- Linux / Raspberry Pi: Raw_Ham_Lite_Logger_Linux.zip
- Guide: https://github.com/RawHamRadio/rawham-lite-logger/raw/main/Guide.pdf

What it does

Everything I wanted at the picnic table, and nothing I didn't.

Built to ride light

I do a lot of my activations off the back of my Can-Am Spyder. There's no room on a bike for a big laptop and a tangle of cables, so I built a logger that packs light. A tablet, a radio, a wire in a tree or a Hamstick, and you're on the air. If it fits in a saddlebag, it'll run this.

Parks and summits as you type

Just type the number. 1234 turns into US-1234. Works with no signal.

Spot yourself

POTA and WWBOTA spots go straight out. GMA and WWFF go through the cluster. QRT too, with one tap.

Who's that?

Names and QTH from an offline US and Canada callbook, or QRZ if you have it. Saved for when you're out of range.

Worked before

Pulls your history from QRZ or an ADIF file so you know who's a new one.

Park to park

Log a P2P with several parks and it writes one ADIF record per park, the way POTA wants it.

Gets your log home

POTA-ready ADIF with the right file names. Send to QRZ, ACLog, Log4OM, HRD, Wavelog and others.

Radio control

Reads band and mode from your rig. Yaesu, Icom, Xiegu and more. USB-OTG on Android.

Dark mode

Easy on the eyes when you're working the night shift.

Installing

About two minutes. Do the first setup at home where you have internet, then it works in the field with no signal.

Windows 10 / 11

  1. Download the Windows setup above and open it.
  2. If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. It says that for anything that isn't from a big company.
  3. Click Next, Install, Finish. Settings opens by itself the first time.

Android

  1. Open this page on the tablet or phone and tap the Android button.
  2. Tap the file when it's done. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  3. Tap Install, open it, and tap Settings.

Linux

  1. Grab the Linux files from the release page.
  2. Open a terminal in that folder and type bash install-linux.sh.
  3. Start it from your app menu.

It tells you when there's an update. If the version number in the corner turns red, there's a new one waiting. On Windows it updates with one click.

Your log stays yours

Your log and settings stay on your own computer or tablet. Nothing gets sent to me. Your QRZ login is only used to talk to QRZ. Please don't share your own rawhams_lite_data.json or rawhams_lite.db files, they hold your settings and passwords.
